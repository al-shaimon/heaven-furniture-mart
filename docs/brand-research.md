## 4. Product Offerings & Category Breakdown

Heaven Furniture Mart does not sell flat-pack items. Their core offerings span five distinct bespoke domains:

1. **Living Room:**
   - Hand-carved solid Chittagong Teak (Segun) sofas
   - Modern tufted Chesterfield sofas & love seats
   - Custom Italian marble-top coffee tables and nesting tables
   - Wall-mounted TV units, media consoles, and credenzas
2. **Bedroom:**
   - Royal and modern platform beds with velvet / leatherette upholstered headboards and brass/gold geometric inlays
   - Custom sliding-door and mirrored wardrobes (built to ceiling height)
   - Vanity dressing tables with LED ring mirrors and ergonomic seating
   - Matching bedside drawers and side cabinets
3. **Dining:**
   - Imported natural marble and solid teak dining tables (6-seater to 12-seater)
   - Hand-carved dining chairs with embroidered velvet backings
   - Custom glass-front curio display cabinets and buffet sideboards
4. **Office & Study:**
   - Executive mahogany and teak desks with cable management
   - Custom bookshelf libraries and display shelving
   - Ergonomic boardroom tables and luxury seating
5. **Bespoke / Custom Architecture:**
   - 100% custom-designed woodwork built according to architectural floor plans
   - Exact sizing for apartments and villas across Chattogram
   - Selection of wood species (Chittagong Teak/Segun, Mahogany, Oak veneers, Gorjon) and imported upholstery fabrics

## 9. Proposed Visual Design Direction

### Aesthetic Concept:
**"Luxury Interior Atelier Meets Editorial Architecture"**
- A high-end sanctuary aesthetic: warm, calm, generous whitespace, confident editorial typography, and authentic materiality.
- Avoid generic dark SaaS themes (no glowing purple borders, neon gradients, or floating cards).
- Grounded in authentic brand colors: **Deep Forest Slate/Teal** (from their official logo: `#1E332F`), **Warm Ecru/Parchment** (`#FBF9F5` / `#F4EFEA`), **Charcoal Ebony** (`#121615`), and subtle **Muted Champagne Gold** (`#C5A869`) used sparingly for fine hairline accents and status badges.

### Typography System:
- **Display Serif (Headings):** `Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond` (via `next/font/google` for zero layout shift and 100 Lighthouse performance).
- **Body & Functional UI:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` for crisp legibility across all screen densities.

## 10. Technical & Performance Risk Analysis

1. **Image Weight & LCP (Largest Contentful Paint):**
   - *Risk:* Multiple high-resolution photography assets could slow page load or trigger layout shift.
   - *Mitigation:* Convert assets to modern WebP/AVIF format with explicit aspect ratios; prioritize hero image with `priority={true}` in `next/image`; apply lazy-loading with blur placeholders for below-the-fold assets.
2. **Next.js 16 App Router & Server Components:**
   - *Risk:* Excessive `"use client"` directives can bloat JavaScript bundle and drop Lighthouse score.
   - *Mitigation:* Keep 90%+ of the page as pure Server Components. Only mobile drawer toggle, consultation modal, or micro-interactive tabs will be client components.
3. **Tailwind CSS v4 Integration:**
   - *Risk:* New `@tailwindcss/postcss` and `@theme` syntax in Tailwind v4.
   - *Mitigation:* Define design tokens cleanly in `globals.css` using CSS custom properties (`--color-brand-teal`, etc.) mapped to `@theme inline`.
4. **Mobile Responsiveness & Viewport Padding:**
   - *Risk:* Elaborate furniture grids overflowing on narrow 320px–390px mobile viewports.
   - *Mitigation:* Mobile-first responsive fluid grids (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4`), strict `overflow-x-hidden`, minimum 48px touch targets for phone and consultation buttons.
