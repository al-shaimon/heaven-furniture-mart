## 1. Exact Brand Positioning

* **What Heaven Furniture Mart Is:** Chattogram's premier bespoke furniture atelier and interior styling studio.
* **What They Are NOT:** They are not a discount flat-pack warehouse, an off-the-shelf mass-production retailer, or an anonymous online catalog.
* **Core Brand Philosophy:** *"Designed. Crafted. Customized."*
* **Core Campaign Anchor:** *"Furniture, Crafted Around You."*
* **Brand Character:** Editorial, calm, confident, warm, established, authentic, and architectural.

---

## 2. Target Customer Profile

* **Primary Persona:** Discerning homeowners, apartment buyers, and villa owners in Chattogram (Khulshi, Nasirabad, Panchlaish, Agrabad, Halishahar, Chandgaon).
* **The Buyer Context:** Moving into a new residence or renovating an ancestral family home. They appreciate fine woodwork, natural marble, rich velvet, and tailored finishes.
* **Secondary Persona:** Architects, interior designers, and corporate executives needing custom-dimensioned boardroom tables, executive desks, and bespoke reception styling.

---

## 3. Primary Customer Problem

1. **The Dimension Trap:** Mass-market ready-made furniture comes in rigid, pre-set sizes that rarely fit modern apartment layouts—leaving clumsy dead zones or obstructing doorways and windows.
2. **Disposable Quality:** Commercial chain stores increasingly use low-density particle boards, paper foils, and synthetic foams that sag or chip within a few seasons.
3. **The Compromise of Mass Production:** Homeowners cannot choose the exact timber species, cushion firmness, fabric weave, or custom details they desire.
4. **Trust Deficit:** Discerning buyers fear commissioning local unorganized carpenters due to lack of quality control, missing warranties, and no physical showroom accountability.

---

## 4. Primary Value Proposition

> **"We don't pull furniture off a shelf. Every piece is custom-designed around your room's exact dimensions, handcrafted from seasoned hardwoods like authentic Chittagong Teak, and finished with master detail—backed by 20+ years of heritage and a physical flagship showroom in Agrabad."**

## 7. Intentionally Excluded Elements (Anti-Patterns)

1. **NO E-Commerce Shopping Cart or Instant Buy Buttons:** Custom bespoke furniture cannot be ordered with an impulsive, anonymous checkout.
2. **NO Fake Markdown Pricing:** Avoid tacky *"Was ৳150,000, Now ৳79,999"* banners. Real luxury bespoke furniture is priced transparently according to wood species, dimensions, and materials.
3. **NO Fake Stock Testimonials:** No generic AI-generated faces pretending to be Chattogram residents. Proof comes from real photographs of the Managing Director, the team, the showroom, and the BFMA 2024 award.
4. **NO SaaS Visual Gimmicks:** No neon purple glow, no floating glassmorphism blobs, no cursor trails, no autoplay video blaring audio.
5. **NO Catalog SKU Overload:** No endless pages of 400 thumbnail products. Focus on curated spatial vignettes.

## 10. Bilingual Content Strategy & WhatsApp Conversion Architecture

### 1. The Dual-Tone Philosophy
Rather than relying on literal machine translation, the platform employs a dual-tone strategy tailored to two key audience segments:
* **Natural Conversational Bengali (Primary for Local Homeowners):**
  - Uses everyday Bangladeshi showroom phrases that resonate with local families visiting Agrabad.
  - Examples:
    - *"আপনার জায়গার মাপ অনুযায়ী ফার্নিচার"* (Bespoke furniture sized to your floor plan).
    - *"শোরুমে এসে নিজের চোখে দেখে পছন্দ করুন"* (Visit the showroom to touch and inspect in person).
    - *"দাম আর সাইজ জানতে আমাদের মেসেজ করুন"* (Direct WhatsApp inquiry).
    - *"দক্ষ কারিগরের যত্নে তৈরি"* (Crafted with master artisan care).
* **Refined Editorial English (For Diaspora, Corporate & Architectural Clients):**
  - Tailored for Chittagong diaspora planning home setup, corporate office commissions, and interior architects.
  - Uses authoritative architectural terminology (*"Architectural Joinery"*, *"Custom Spatial Dimensions"*, *"Seasoned Hardwood Atelier"*).

### 2. Interactive Estimation & WhatsApp Structured Quote Handoff
- The bespoke customizer (`BespokeCostEstimator.tsx` / `RequestQuoteModal.tsx`) bridges the gap between digital curiosity and physical consultation.
- When a user selects a room type, wood selection, and dimensions, the engine generates an estimate and composes an automated, pre-formatted WhatsApp message:
  ```
  আসসালামু আলাইকুম হেভেন ফার্নিচার মার্ট,
  আমি আপনাদের ওয়েবসাইট থেকে একটি কাস্টম ফার্নিচারের কোটেশন জানতে চাই:
  • ফার্নিচার ক্যাটাগরি: লিভিং রুম / সোফা সেট
  • কাঠের পছন্দ: চিটাগাং সেগুন
  • আনুমানিক বাজেট: ৳৮০,০০০ - ৳১,২০,০০০
  অনুগ্রহ করে বিস্তারিত ও শোরুম ভিজিটের সময় জানাবেন।
  ```
- This completely removes customer friction: no typing required on mobile, immediately opening WhatsApp with the exact specs ready to send.
